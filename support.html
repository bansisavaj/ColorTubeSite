Common questions

How do I play? Tap a tube, then tap another tube to pour. Liquid pours onto the same colour or into an empty tube. Fill every tube with a single colour to win.

I'm stuck. With Prevent Dead Ends on (Settings), every level can always be completed. You can also use Hint, Undo, Extra Tube, or Restart, which is always free.

How do I restore Remove Ads? Open Settings in the game and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

How do I change my ad privacy choices? Open Settings in the game and tap Privacy Choices (shown where required by law).

Is my progress saved? Yes, automatically on your device. Deleting the app deletes your progress.
