ColorTube: Water Sort Puzzles Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

What we collect

ColorTube has no accounts and no servers of its own. Your game progress, settings, helpers and daily streak are stored only on your device.

Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads, Google may collect device and usage information such as IP address, device model, operating-system version, app interactions with ads, approximate location derived from IP, and advertising-related identifiers where permitted. We do not request App Tracking Transparency permission and the app does not access your advertising identifier (IDFA) for tracking. Where required by law (for example in the EEA, UK and some US states), you are asked for consent through Google's consent message, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings › Privacy Choices. Google's policy: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Purchases

The optional Remove Ads purchase is processed by Apple. We receive only a confirmation that the purchase is valid; we never see your payment details.

Children

ColorTube is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices

You can reset ad consent in Settings › Privacy Choices (when shown), limit ad tracking in iOS Settings, or remove ads with the one-time purchase. Deleting the app deletes all locally stored game data.
